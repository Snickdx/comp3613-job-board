# **Job Board Requirements & Interview Traceability**

# **Special Feature Callout**

* **Direct Employer-to-Applicant In-Platform Messaging & Status-Triggered Notifications (Product Feature)**  
  * A dedicated communication channel allowing employers to directly update applicants during the application review phase (e.g., to request clarifications or additional supporting materials to make their resume fit the criteria), tagged along with automated push notifications  specifically alerting graduates the moment their application moves from being `submitted` to being under `review`.  
  * Across all three interviews with Graduate application,Employer and Administrator, the primary issue is a communication barrier or "black hole", post-submission. Graduates tend to feel unattended after they have applied , while employers lack an integrated way to ask quick clarifying questions before making a shortlist/hire decision.

# **Key Traceability Notes**

* **Public Acceptance Criteria Reconciliation:** Sharing `docs/ACCEPTANCE.md` resolved several critical domain rules:  
  * **Apply Modes (FR-01, FR-04):** Listings explicitly distinguish `external-link` vs. `on-platform collect`, preventing fake in-app tracking for external redirects.  
  * **Strict Lifecycle Ownership (FR-05, NFR-02):** Only the owning employer may advance an application's state (`submitted -> reviewed -> shortlisted -> rejected | hired`); Administrators cannot modify application statuses.  
  * **Admin Applicant Export (FR-07, NFR-06):** Administrators can export applicant packages for forwarding, aligning with their interview role of connecting graduates with employers.  
  * **Program Eligibility (FR-03, NFR-02):** Graduate eligibility in the starter baseline is specifically enforced for `DCIT` graduates (blocking `OTHER`).  
* **Transcript Speaker Attribution Note:** In Item 3 (Employer) at 02:49 AM, the log labels the speaker as `vector:job-board:administrator`, though the text describes the employer's perspective of filling out the job form. Both the 02:49 AM and 02:50 AM entries were cross-referenced under Employer submission requirements.  
* **Cross-Role Consensus:** Both the Administrator (08:26 AM) and Employer (08:28 AM) independently emphasized blocking duplicate applications to the same job while permitting applications across different roles (08:25 AM), making FR-04 a strict business rule.
